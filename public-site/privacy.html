Privacy Policy

How Neko Academy collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Neko Academy collects, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, username, and profile details you provide.
  • Payment information: processed by our payment provider — we never store your full card number on our servers.
  • Usage data: course progress, quiz results, and community activity, used to power your dashboard and certificates.
  • Device & log data: IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used for security and analytics.

2. How we use your information

We use your data to deliver the Service (tracking course progress, issuing certificates, processing payments), to communicate with you about your account or purchases, and to improve course content based on aggregate usage patterns.

3. What we don't do

We don't sell your personal data to third parties. We don't share your community messages or personalized messages outside the platform except as required by law.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

5. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting support.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and access controls. No system is 100% secure, so we encourage using a strong, unique password.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be directed to our support team or to info@nekoacademy.com.
