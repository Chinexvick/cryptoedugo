Refund Policy

Straightforward terms for cancellations and refunds.

Last updated: August 1, 2026

14-day guarantee

If you're not satisfied with a course, you can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase, provided you have completed less than 30% of the course content. This gives you enough time to properly evaluate whether a course is right for you, while remaining fair to instructors.

How to request a refund

  • Go to Order History in your dashboard and select "Request refund" on the relevant order, or
  • Send a message via Contact with your order number and the reason for your request.

Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days back to your original payment method.

Bundles & Learning Paths

Refunds on bundled Learning Paths are calculated against the full bundle price, not the individual course prices, and follow the same 14-day / 30%-completion rule applied to the bundle as a whole.

What isn't refundable

  • Courses where more than 30% of lessons have been marked complete.
  • Certificates that have already been issued and verified.
  • Promotional or heavily discounted bundle purchases explicitly marked "final sale" at checkout.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a chargeback with your bank — we resolve the vast majority of billing issues directly and faster than a chargeback process. Accounts with unresolved chargebacks may be suspended.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, reach out to support — a real person reads every message.
