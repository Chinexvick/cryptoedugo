Reward accrual math

In this lesson you'll implement the reward-per-second accrual formula used by the staking vault, and understand why naive implementations lead to rounding exploits.

We'll walk through the math on a whiteboard first, then translate it directly into Solidity — comparing a vulnerable first attempt against the hardened final version used in production vaults.
